# Apple Experience Skills v2.2.0

v2.2 strengthens visual authenticity, adaptive composition, interaction responsiveness, and media storytelling.

## Highlights

- Neutral-first, content-led design guidance that rejects arbitrary neon and gradient AI aesthetics without banning legitimate brand, product, semantic, or accessible color.
- Stronger icon-semantic and visualization discipline, including safeguards against decorative icon clutter, fake comparison graphics, and unnecessary dashboardification.
- Desktop / Tablet / Phone composition-family reasoning instead of simple layout scaling.
- Input-Coupled Motion and Follow-the-Hand guidance for responsive drag, swipe, scrub, and scroll-linked interactions.
- Optional Auto-Advancing Peek Rail guidance with user-control arbitration.
- Bounded Scroll Handoff for vertical-scroll-driven horizontal storytelling that naturally returns to normal document flow.
- Visual Authenticity enforcement across Experience Design, Adaptive Structure, Motion Physics, Direct Manipulation, and Visual Media Composition.

Visual Authenticity Contract, Input-Coupled Motion, Auto-Advancing Peek Rail, and Bounded Scroll Handoff are Skill-owned concepts, not official Apple terminology or requirements.

## Package

The public distribution contains the installable Skills, required Tier 2 references, three cross-cutting contracts, README, release notes, and license. Development tests, evaluations, audit reports, scanner tooling, screenshots, and internal logs are not distributed.
